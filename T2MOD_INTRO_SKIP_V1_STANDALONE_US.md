# TMNT2 T2Mod Intro Skip v1 Standalone US

US/NA x86 EXE-only source branch for the standalone Intro Skip package.

- Keeps the normal save/load check.
- Skips the Konami logo, studio logo, and startup opening movie.
- Leaves title attract movies, story movies, and all other movies unchanged.
- Includes shared buffered runtime diagnostics.
- Does not include MSAA, widescreen, difficulty, or playable Slashuur changes.
- Does not rebuild or replace `TMNT.DAT` or any LPAC resource.

Source baseline: `dabccc3e3dc02c8a01eac1638fe1aeb589e2723b`.

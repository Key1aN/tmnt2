# TMNT2 T2Mod Brutal Difficulties v3 Standalone US

US/NA x86 EXE-only source branch for the standalone final three-difficulty package.

- Adds Very Hard, Extreme, and Souls Like after the original Hard option.
- Player damage received scales to 2.5x, 4x, and 6x respectively.
- Enemy HP scales to 1.5x, 2x, and 2.5x respectively.
- Includes the final extended aggression, attack interval, and super-armor tuning.
- Maps extended modes safely to the original Hard AI/parameter table row.
- Keeps Nexus mode forced to Normal and preserves the existing save layout.
- Includes shared buffered runtime diagnostics.
- Does not include Intro Skip, MSAA, widescreen, or playable Slashuur changes.
- Does not rebuild or replace `TMNT.DAT` or any LPAC resource.

Source baseline: `dabccc3e3dc02c8a01eac1638fe1aeb589e2723b`.
